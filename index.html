About the Numbers

Season stats
The season-long stats in these tables (points, minutes, usage, pace, ratings and so on) are from this season.
+/- columns
Compare each player's or team's last 5 games with their season numbers, so you can spot who is trending up or down.

Badges & Filters

PS
A bright green "PS" badge next to a player's name means projected starter.
DK Slate / FD Slate
Badge colors: orange M = Main, green L = Late, blue S = Showdown, black E = Early (same colors for both sites). Use the slate drop-down to show only the games on a specific site's slate (Main, Early, Late or Showdown).
DK Pos / FD Pos
Position filters for each site. DraftKings players can be eligible at more than one spot (e.g. PG/SG); G matches PG or SG, F matches SF or PF, and UTIL matches everyone. FanDuel lists a single position per player. Both filters can be combined.

Schedule

Favorite / Spread
The favored team and the size of the spread.
Game Total
The over/under for the game.
Road Total / Home Total
Implied point total for the road / home team, derived from the spread and game total.

Schedule Notes

Spot / Notes
The scheduling situation for each team on tonight's slate, combined into one line per team: back-to-backs (first or second night), 3 games in 4 nights, rest edges, travel and so on.
B2B tag
A red B2B tag next to a team's name on the Schedule and Team Totals tabs means it is on the second night of a back-to-back. Hover it for the details.
Colors
Amber is the first night of a back-to-back. Red is the second night or a heavy stretch (3 in 4, 4 in 5). Green is a rest edge. Blue is travel. Gray is anything else.

Team Totals & Pace

Line / Total
The team's spread and implied team total.
PPG
Points scored per game.
+/-
The change in the stat to its left: the last 5 games minus the season number. A positive number means the team or player is trending up recently.
O-Rat
Offensive rating rank (1–30) — points scored per 100 possessions.
Pace
Pace rank (1–30) — possessions per 48 minutes.
Reb %
Rank (1–30) for rebound rate — share of available rebounds the team collects.
Opp Pace, Opp Reb %
The same ranks for this team's opponent.
Opp 3PAtt / Opp 3P %
Ranks (1–30) for the three-point attempts and three-point percentage the opposing defense allows.
Opp PAPG
Rank (1–30) for points allowed per game by the opposing defense.
Opp D-Rat
Rank (1–30) for the opponent's defensive rating — points allowed per 100 possessions.
Colors
Blue is good for the offense, red is bad. For the team's own stats and for Opp Pace, rank 1 is blue. For the opponent's defensive stats (Opp Reb %, 3PAtt, 3P %, PAPG, D-Rat), rank 1 is red, the toughest matchup.
P-Pace
Projected pace for tonight's game, combining both teams' pace and the spread.

Color Coding

Every colored stat is ranked against the rest of the table and shaded on a continuous scale — deep blue for the best matchups/performances, deep orange/red for the worst, with lighter shades in between. The scale is calculated automatically from that day's data.

WorstAverageElite

For a few stats (like an opponent's rebounding rate), a lower raw number is the better outcome — those are colored so blue always means "good" and red always means "bad."
